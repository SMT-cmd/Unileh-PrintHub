Privacy Policy

Your data security and privacy are our top priorities.

Introduction

AfriNet Hub is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our services at afrinethub.com.ng.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our services, including:

  • Personal Details: Name, phone number, and matriculation number.
  • Documents: Files uploaded for printing are processed and stored securely.
  • Portal assistance: We process the contact and request details you supply. We never need your university portal password.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely for:

  • Processing and delivering your print, gadget, housing, and project/seminar orders and requests.
  • Providing requested student portal assistance.
  • Communicating order and request status via WhatsApp.
  • Improving our platform's user experience.

Security Measures

We use technical and organizational controls to protect submitted information. Please do not share passwords or financial PINs. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Contact

If you have questions about our privacy practices, contact us at support@afrinethub.com.ng.
