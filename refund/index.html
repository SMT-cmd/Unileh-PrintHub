Refund Policy

We aim for crystal-clear prints, every single time.

Eligibility for Refunds

Refunds or reprints are only available for **printing defects** such as incorrect coloring, missing pages, or poor print quality that makes the document unreadable.

Claim Period

You must submit your claim via WhatsApp within **24 hours** of collecting your prints. Please provide your Order ID and a photo of the defect.

Processing Time

Once your claim is reviewed and approved, refunds or reprints will be processed within **3–5 business days**.

Non-Refundable Items

Portal assistance services, once performed, are not eligible for refunds. Advertisements are non-refundable once they have been posted on the platform.
