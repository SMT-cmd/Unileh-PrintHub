About Unilesh Print Hub

Empowering Unilesa students with reliable and fast academic services.

Our Mission

Unilesh Print Hub was created with a simple mission: to take the stress out of academic life for University of Ilesha students. We understand the pressure of deadlines and the complexity of portal management. Our platform is designed to provide crystal-clear prints and expert portal assistance so you can focus on what matters most—your grades.

Powered by AfriNet Hub

As part of the AfriNet Hub network, we leverage cutting-edge technology to provide a secure, real-time platform. From Appwrite-powered file storage to real-time Firestore updates, every part of our system is built for speed and security.

Our Services

  • Print-on-Demand: High-quality Black & White and Color prints with easy file uploads.
  • Portal Assistance: Expert help with exam cards, library passes, and login issues.
  • Campus Updates: Real-time news ticker and announcements for the Unilesa community.
  • Business Adverts: Providing a platform for local businesses to reach the student body.
