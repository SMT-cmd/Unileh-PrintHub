Privacy Policy

Your data security and privacy are our top priorities.

Introduction

Unilesh Print Hub (powered by AfriNet Hub) is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our services at unilesaservices.com.ng.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our services, including:

  • Personal Details: Name, phone number, and matriculation number.
  • Documents: Files uploaded for printing are processed and stored temporarily on Cloudinary.
  • Portal Credentials: Usernames and passwords provided for portal assistance are used for the session only and are not stored permanently.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely for:

  • Processing and delivering your print orders.
  • Providing requested student portal assistance.
  • Communicating order status via WhatsApp.
  • Improving our platform's user experience.

Security Measures

We use Firebase's secure database and Cloudinary's encrypted storage to protect your data. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
